Research date: 2026-09-27. Method note: the egress proxy blocked full-page fetches of businesswire.com, qoppac.blogspot.com (Carver), medium.com, dev.to, traderank.ai, iweaver.ai, awealthofcommonsense.com and travisgiffin.com. ## 1. Evidence on the minority of profitable retail traders: shared traits and practices

### Takeaway
Large-sample academic studies (Brazil 2013–2015 cohort; Taiwan 1992–2006) show that fewer than about 1–3% of active day traders are predictably profitable, and experience alone does not make them profitable. These studies predate 2023 but remain the best evidence. The winners in real-money competitions from 2024–2025 (the US Investing Championship) are concentrated, high-return stock traders, mostly Minervini students. They are survivorship-selected, and the published results give no median or loss data.

### Cited Findings
- **Brazil (Chague, De-Losso, Giovannetti, "Day Trading for a Living?", SSRN 2019/2020 WP):** covers every individual who started day trading Brazilian equity index mini-futures from 2013 to 2015 (19,646 people). Of the 1,551 who persisted for more than 300 trading days, 97% lost money net of fees. Only 1.1% earned more than the Brazilian minimum wage and 0.5% more than a bank teller's starting salary, and they took great risk to do it. The top earner made about $310/day. [SNIPPET] — [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3423101); [QuantPedia summary](https://quantpedia.com/retail-day-trading-is-an-uphill-battle/)
- **Taiwan (Barber, Lee, Liu, Odean):** Taiwan Stock Exchange data from 1992 to 2006. Day trading was about 17% of volume. Day traders lost an average of 23.9 bp/day net of fees, and aggregate day-trader performance was negative in 14 of 15 years. "Predictably profitable" traders (past profits plus more than 40 days of experience) were under 3% of day traders and produced only 9.81% of day-trading volume. Under 1% of the population earns predictable profits. [SNIPPET] — [Barber et al. "Do Individual Day Traders Make Money?"](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/Day%20Traders/Day%20Trade%20040330.pdf); [Cross-section of speculator skill (J. Financial Markets)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1386418113000190)
- **Learning:** day-trading activity rises with experience, but "more trading experience does not contribute to higher trading profits." Most losers keep trading instead of quitting once they learn their ability. [SNIPPET] — [Barber et al., "Do Day Traders Rationally Learn About Their Ability?"](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/Day%20Traders/Day%20Trading%20and%20Learning%20110217.pdf); [Tradicted summary](https://www.tradicted.com/research/barber-learning-2020/)
- **US Investing Championship 2024 (final, reported Jan 27 2025):** 451 competitors, each tracking a real-money account. In the $1M+ stock division, J Law (Hong Kong) returned +353.9%, a new record above Minervini's +334.8%, and is described as Minervini's student. Judy Lai placed 2nd with +273.8% and Deepak Uppal 3rd with +153.2%. [SNIPPET, press release; entrant returns are self-submitted brokerage statements] — [Business Wire, 2025-01-27](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250127956815/en/2024-United-States-Investing-Championship-Final-Results)
- **USIC 2025 first half (reported Jul 30 2025):** 567 competitors. In the $1M+ stock division, J Law returned +184.3%, Joseph Pullig +53.9% and George Tkaczuk (RCM Wealth Advisors) +49.3%. [SNIPPET] — [Business Wire, 2025-07-30](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250730525524/en/2025-United-States-Investing-Championship-First-Half-Results)
- Snippets also report that Bob Weissman set a record of +115.4% in an "Enhanced Growth +$1M" division of the 2025 USIC. [SNIPPET, secondary aggregator] — [TradersUnion](https://tradersunion.com/news/market-voices/show/1333620-championship-record-win/)
- Minervini said that 27 of the 79 leaders listed as of 2024-04-30 were his "Private Access" clients, including 1st place (Leos Mikulka, +317.8%) and 2nd place (James Hatzigiannis, +224.8%). He also said 1st place in 3 of 4 divisions through May 2024 (435 entrants) went to his clients. [SELF-REPORTED marketing post; SNIPPET] — [Minervini on X (Apr 2024)](https://x.com/markminervini/status/1798078883163681006); [Minervini on X (May 2024)](https://x.com/markminervini/status/1805966555156496608)
- **Unknown Market Wizards (Schwager, 2020) as summarized:** the recurring themes are strict risk management with small per-trade risk and stops, discipline and patience, reducing exposure during drawdowns, and methods that differ from trader to trader. [SNIPPET, secondary book summaries] — [Shortform summary](https://www.shortform.com/summary/unknown-market-wizards-summary-jack-d-schwager); [Elearnmarkets summary](https://www.elearnmarkets.com/school/units/unknown-market-wizards)

### Inferences
- The base rate is brutal: roughly 97–99% of high-frequency retail day traders lose. A small daily bot should be built so it avoids the known failure modes (overtrading, fees, intraday noise) rather than trying to join the tiny day-trader elite.
- The USIC winners of 2024–2025 hold concentrated growth-stock positions (Minervini-style SEPA/VCP breakouts with tight stops). Their huge returns come alongside huge variance, and the competition publishes winners, not loser distributions, so this is survivorship evidence and not proof of a transferable edge.
- Profitability in the Taiwan data *persists* for a small group. So a bot's live track record, not its backtest, should decide whether it is allowed to scale (see Davey's incubation practice below).

### Gaps
- I found no study from 2023–2026 that quantifies the holding period, risk per trade, win rate and payoff, or journaling of profitable retail traders. The Brazil and Taiwan papers cover 1992–2015 samples.
- I could not fetch current broker disclosures (for example, ESMA-mandated "X% of retail CFD accounts lose money" figures), so no dated percentages are given.
- USIC does not publish median or loss statistics. I found none.

## 2. How well-known systematic retail practitioners run their systems now

### Takeaway
Current practitioners agree on a process: a few simple rules, volatility-scaled position sizing, diversification across many instruments or systems, out-of-sample and incubation testing before real money, and scheduled (often annual) performance reviews against a benchmark.

### Cited Findings
- **Robert Carver** (former AHL portfolio manager, retail futures trader) restarted his annual performance review in April 2025, covering only his systematic futures portfolio for the UK tax year ending 5 April 2025. He had skipped the previous year's review because covering the whole portfolio had become too much work. By October 2025 his system was reported up about 5.5% year to date after recovering from a summer drawdown. [SNIPPET; blog fetch blocked; SELF-REPORTED] — [qoppac 2025-04 annual update](https://qoppac.blogspot.com/2025/04/annual-performance-update-returneth.html); [qoppac Oct 2025](https://qoppac.blogspot.com/2025/10/)
- **Andreas Clenow ("Stocks on the Move") rules as implemented by practitioners:** rank stocks by the annualized exponential-regression slope over 90 days multiplied by R². Size each position so it moves about 10 bp of equity per day, using AccountValue × 0.001 / ATR(20) (risk parity). The rules rotate into the top-ranked stocks. Clenow deliberately calls stock trend following "momentum" because its implementation differs from futures trend following. [SNIPPET; community implementations, not a 2025 interview] — [QuantConnect forum](https://www.quantconnect.com/forum/discussion/4119/implementation-of-a-simple-stock-trend-following-strategy-inspired-by-andreas-clenow-039-s-quot-stocks-on-the-move-quot/); [Teddy Koker Python implementation](https://teddykoker.com/2019/05/momentum-strategy-from-stocks-on-the-move-in-python/); [Better System Trader ep. 13](https://bettersystemtrader.com/013-andreas-clenow/)
- **Kevin Davey** (30+ years algo trading) says the process matters more than any one strategy. He keeps parameters few and uses true out-of-sample tests, walk-forward analysis, tests across multiple markets and timeframes, and Monte Carlo simulation. Strategies that pass are then incubated live **without real money for 6–12 months**, and he runs many simple systems as a portfolio. [SNIPPET; secondary summaries plus a 2025 IBKR podcast] — [IBKR Campus "Algo Advantage 036 – It's All About Process"](https://ibkrcampus.com/campus/ibkr-quant-news/algo-advantage-036-kevin-davey-part-i-its-all-about-process-in-algo-trading/); [FinancialWisdomTV summary](https://www.financialwisdomtv.com/post/building-winning-algorithmic-trading-systems-kevin-davey-s-complete-framework-for-trading-success); [forex.in.rs summary](https://www.forex.in.rs/simple-systems-diversified/)
- Davey's site also advertises a "Spring 2025 Kevin GPT" product, which shows even veteran systematic traders adding LLM tooling to research workflows. [SNIPPET; commercial] — [kjtradingsystems.com](https://kjtradingsystems.com/2025-05kevingpt.html)
- **Meb Faber** has not changed the core model since 2007: trend-follow five asset classes with a 200-day / 10-month moving average and move to cash when the trend breaks. The known cost is whipsaw. Faber noted in April 2025 that about 70–80% of the market's best and worst days occur below the 200-day MA. [SNIPPET] — [Concretum Group GTAA automation](https://concretumgroup.substack.com/p/global-tactical-asset-allocation); [Meb Faber on X, Apr 2025](https://x.com/MebFaber/status/1910023274727391375)
- **Mark Minervini:** results are covered in section 1. His students dominate the USIC $1M+ stock division in 2024–2025. [SELF-REPORTED plus press release]

### Inferences
- Rules a small bot can adopt directly: (a) volatility-normalized sizing (Clenow's 10 bp/day per position, or Carver-style vol targeting); (b) a small parameter count; (c) paper incubation before live capital (Davey: 6–12 months; a bot might accept a shorter but explicit minimum); (d) a scheduled review against a buy-and-hold benchmark; (e) diversification across several uncorrelated simple rules instead of one optimized rule.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch the full text of Carver's 2025 review (returns versus the SG CTA index, costs, instrument count) or any 2024–2026 Clenow interview. Larry Connors' post-2023 research updates were not found.

## 3. Out-of-sample performance of popular retail strategies in 2023–2026

### Takeaway
The evidence is thin and mostly from vendors. RSI(2)-style mean reversion on US indices reportedly stayed profitable in 2024 and weakened in 2025. Faber's 200-day and dual-momentum/GEM models suffer whipsaw in sharp V-shaped selloffs such as April 2025, but TAA users reported strong 2025 returns overall. I could not retrieve AllocateSmartly's per-strategy 2023–2026 numbers (paywalled or blocked).

### Cited Findings
- **RSI(2) (Connors):** a QuantifiedStrategies backtest on SPY from about 1993 to 2025 turned $100k into about $1.7M over 33 years (about 9% annualized) while in the market only about 27% of the time. Average gain was 0.57% per trade, the win rate about 75% and max drawdown about 23%. For recent years, 2024 had an 85.9% win rate and a profit factor above 2, while 2025 was harder but still profitable. Win rates fell below 60% in 2008 and March 2020, and a VIX < 25 regime filter helps. [SNIPPET; vendor backtest, not audited live results] — [QuantifiedStrategies RSI(2)](https://www.quantifiedstrategies.com/rsi-2-strategy/); [QuantifiedStrategies RSI2 on SPY](https://www.quantifiedstrategies.com/rsi2-on-spy/); [QS substack RSI QQQ](https://quantifiedstrategies.substack.com/p/rsi-trading-strategy-for-qqqrsi-qqq)
- **Faber GTAA / 200-day:** the core weakness is whipsaw, where price crosses the 10-month MA repeatedly. The model is unchanged since 2007. [SNIPPET] — [Concretum Group](https://concretumgroup.substack.com/p/global-tactical-asset-allocation); [Freefincal backtest](https://freefincal.com/market-timing-with-ten-month-moving-average-tactical-asset-allocation-backtest-part-2/). A Ben Carlson post from October 2025, "Trend Following in a Bubble," discusses trend following in the 2025 market, but only its title and snippet were visible. [SNIPPET, fetch blocked] — [A Wealth of Common Sense, Oct 2025](https://awealthofcommonsense.com/2025/10/trend-following-in-a-bubble/)
- **GEM / dual momentum:** GEM puts 100% in one asset using a single 12-month lookback, so it carries high "specification risk" (sensitivity to exact lookback and rebalance date) and periodic whipsaw. AllocateSmartly tracks Faber GTAA, Antonacci Dual Momentum, Accelerating Dual Momentum, Composite Dual Momentum and others. [SNIPPET] — [AllocateSmartly Composite Dual Momentum](https://allocatesmartly.com/antonaccis-composite-dual-momentum/); [AllocateSmartly Accelerating Dual Momentum](https://allocatesmartly.com/taa-strategy-accelerating-dual-momentum/); [AllocateSmartly list of strategies](https://allocatesmartly.com/list-of-strategies/)
- **A retail user following AllocateSmartly models:** three combined TAA models returned 20.25% in 2025 across four accounts. [SELF-REPORTED, single individual, SNIPPET] — [Travis Giffin blog, "2025 > 2026"](https://travisgiffin.com/my-allocatesmartly-tactical-asset-allocation-2025-2026/)

### Inferences
- A long-only daily bot combining a 200-day regime filter with short-term mean-reversion entries (RSI(2)-style) matches the most-documented retail pattern. It should expect degraded win rates in bear and high-VIX regimes and should use a volatility or VIX gate.
- Single-asset, single-lookback rotation (GEM) is fragile to its exact parameters. Blending lookbacks (1/3/6/12-month, as in Faber GTAA 13 or Composite Dual Momentum) or splitting rebalance days reduces that risk.

### Gaps
- No verified 2023–2026 out-of-sample figures (CAGR, drawdown) for GTAA, GEM, VCP breakouts or RSI(2) from an independent tracker. The AllocateSmartly, Portfolio Visualizer and Alpha Architect pages were not retrievable. Minervini/VCP systematic out-of-sample tests were not found.

## 4. Documented results of LLM/AI trading bots with real or paper money, 2025–2026

### Takeaway
Public real-money LLM contests from late 2025 show most frontier models losing money, often heavily, when trading autonomously and at high frequency. Longer-running live benchmarks from 2026 show roughly coin-flip profitability across model-seasons. Individual developers' blogs report bleed from fees and overtrading, and losses from flash-crash exposure.

### Cited Findings
- **Alpha Arena Season 1 (Nof1, Oct–Nov 2025, about $10k real money per model, crypto perps, identical prompts):** Qwen 3 Max won with +22.31%, and DeepSeek V3.1 was the only other model in profit at +4.89%. The losses were GPT (ChatGPT) −$6,267, Gemini −$5,671, Grok −$4,531 and Claude Sonnet −$3,081 on $10,000 each. [SNIPPET; secondary] — [Protos](https://protos.com/llm-crypto-trading-contest-finds-llms-cant-trade-crypto/); [Union Leader / AP-style report](https://www.unionleader.com/news/business/ai-bots-auditioning-for-wall-street-trading-are-mostly-losing/article_47ea31e4-e4b3-4c68-814a-1dee364fb9a2.html); [Nof1](https://nof1.ai/)
- **Alpha Arena Season 1.5 (Nov 19 – Dec 3 2025):** 8 models with $320,000 deployed in total, trading US equities (TSLA, NVDA, MSFT, AMZN, Nasdaq-100) across multiple competition variants. A "Mystery Model" (reported as Grok 4.20) won with a +12.11% aggregate return and $4,844 total. GPT-5.1 was 2nd and Gemini 3 3rd, while some variants of Qwen and DeepSeek were reportedly down about 70%. [SNIPPET; secondary crypto news] — [GNCrypto](https://www.gncrypto.news/news/mystery-model-alpha-arena-season-1-5-winner/); [Euclidean AI blog](https://www.euclideanai.com/blog/llm-crypto-trading)
- **Alpha Arena Season 2:** snippet sources say no Season 2 results had been published as of 2026-08-06, and that Nof1 raised $15M in May 2026 to build a consumer product. [SNIPPET; unverified] — [traderank.ai alternatives, 2026](https://www.traderank.ai/blog/alpha-arena-alternatives-2026); [GitHub research issue](https://github.com/josejuarez96/tradepartner/issues/230)
- **TradeRank live LLM benchmark (since Jan 2026):** 56 LLM competitors each managed $10k of *simulated* capital on live crypto and US stocks over 9 completed seasons and 2,826 trades. Only 46.2% of model-seasons finished profitable. [SNIPPET; paper money; fetch blocked] — [traderank.ai LLM for trading](https://www.traderank.ai/llm-for-trading); [traderank.ai benchmark, Sep 2026](https://www.traderank.ai/llm-trading-benchmark)
- **Individual developer (J. G. Dolejs, Medium, late 2025):** his LLM bot lost 14% in 16 hours during the Oct 29–30 flash crash. After a rewrite it went to paper trading on Nov 2 and returned −3.19% on 2 trades (0% win rate). A separate 9-day run bled equity from $10,808 to $9,852 ("death by a thousand small cuts"). He argues that LLM trading tests need at least about two weeks and small real stakes (roughly $20). [SNIPPET; SELF-REPORTED] — [Medium: 9 days](https://medium.com/@kojott/i-built-an-ai-trading-bot-and-let-it-trade-for-9-days-heres-what-happened-6ceb69ad4d08); [Medium: lost money](https://medium.com/@kojott/my-ai-trading-bot-just-lost-money-heres-why-i-m-excited-about-it-e466c877366b); [Medium: two weeks and twenty dollars](https://medium.com/@kojott/why-llm-trading-backtests-need-two-weeks-and-twenty-dollars-5a19a525a095)
- **DEV Community post, "My LLM Trading Bots Beat the Benchmark. The Rules I Wrote Beforehand Say It Doesn't Count":** a developer pre-registered success criteria and rejected an apparent outperformance because it failed those criteria. Details were not retrievable. [SNIPPET, title only] — [dev.to](https://dev.to/nunc/my-llm-trading-bots-beat-the-benchmark-the-rules-i-wrote-beforehand-say-it-doesnt-count-3455)

### Inferences
- The failures cluster around discretionary, high-frequency, leveraged LLM decisions (crypto perps, intraday). This supports the planned design: an LLM that operates a pre-specified, rules-based, long-only daily system instead of making free-form trade calls.
- A 2–4 week contest is statistically meaningless, since the dispersion between identical-prompt models is huge. Pre-registered evaluation criteria and a benchmark comparison are the practice worth copying.

### Gaps
- No primary Nof1 data (trade counts, fees, leverage, holding periods) could be retrieved. No documented long-running (6+ months) real-money, long-only daily LLM bot results were found.
