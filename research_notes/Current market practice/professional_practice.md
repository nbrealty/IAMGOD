## 1. Strategy / factor performance, 2025 to Sept 2026

### Takeaway
Trend following had its worst 12 months on record into April 2025 (SG Trend -18.7%). It recovered to about +2.4% for full-year 2025 and has had a strong 2026 (trend sub-index about +12.9% YTD through Aug 2026, snippet). Value led equity factors in 2025 and Q1 2026. Momentum led in Q2 2026 and YTD, driven by tech/semis, but it has become crowded and fragile, with sharp unwinds in Jan 2026 and reportedly July/Aug 2026. Low-vol was the weakest factor in Q2 2026. I found no institutional data on RSI(2)-style short-term reversal.

## 2. Risk practices at systematic funds and pod shops

### Takeaway
Pod shops use tiered drawdown stops. At Millennium, a 5% loss halves the PM's capital and a 7.5% loss ends the pod; in practice, risk teams start cutting at about 2.5-3%. Vol-control funds mechanically scale exposure inversely to realized volatility. Crowding and factor exposure are actively monitored, because unwinds of crowded trades caused the worst 2026 quant losses.

### Cited Findings
- Typical two-tier structure: a soft stop at about -5% of allocated capital cuts risk roughly in half, and a hard stop at -7% to -10% liquidates the book. At Millennium, a 5% loss halves capital and a 7.5% loss terminates the pod. Citadel and Point72 reportedly negotiate thresholds per PM. Citadel reportedly did not cut PMs for performance in the March 2025 sell-off. — [Hedge Fund Interview, "Pod Shop Risk Limits"](https://hedgefundinterview.com/pod-shop-risk-limits); [Young and Calculated](https://youngandcalculated.substack.com/p/how-pms-actually-get-fired-at-multi) (snippets; practitioner blogs, not firm disclosures)
- Veteran PMs describe the "real working number" as 2.5-3% from peak, where capital cuts start and the PM goes "on watch". — same sources (snippet)
- Multi-manager platforms managed about $931bn by Q1 2025, nearly a quarter of hedge-fund industry capital. — same search result set (snippet)
- 2025 results: Millennium +10.5%, Citadel Wellington +10.2%, Point72 +18%, ExodusPoint +18%, Schonfeld Strategic +12.5%. Millennium and Citadel lost early in 2025 during the trade war and recovered in H2. The S&P 500 was +16.5% for 2025. — [Hedgeweek](https://www.hedgeweek.com/millennium-and-citadel-post-double-digit-2025-gains-but-lag-smaller-hedge-funds/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/2025-returns-hedge-funds-rolling-234728986.html) (snippets)
- March 2026 volatility reportedly "exposed cracks in the pod-shop playbook". — [HedgeCo, Mar 2026](https://hedgeco.net/news/03/2026/the-multistrategy-performance-reset.html) (headline only; content blocked)
- Vol-control strategies manage an estimated $300-500bn of notional equity exposure, mostly S&P 500 futures. They add exposure when volatility is low and cut it when volatility is high, and a moderate shift in volatility can move tens of billions within 24-48h. In mid-June 2026, 1-month and 3-month realized volatility were both about 16% with a spread of about +0.3pp, so vol-control funds were near neutral. — [Delta n Theta substack](https://deltantheta.substack.com/p/the-hidden-buyers-and-sellers-volatility) (snippet; secondary estimate)
- Volatility targeting is procyclical. It raises leverage in calm markets and forces selling when volatility spikes (a "vol control cascade"). — same; background in [Man Group, "The Impact of Volatility Targeting"](https://www.man.com/insights/the-impact-of-volatility-targeting) and [ECB FSR 2020 box](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/focus/2020/html/ecb.fsrbox202005_02~f6616db9be.en.html) (pre-2025 background)
- Goldman described the Jan 2026 quant losses as coming from crowded positions, high-beta shorts and idiosyncratic moves. — [Bloomberg, 21 Jan 2026](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-21/quants-in-worst-drawdown-since-october-as-crowded-bets-buckle) (snippet)

### Inferences
- Pod-shop logic maps onto a small bot as tiered, pre-committed drawdown rules: for example, halve gross exposure at a -5% equity drawdown and go flat or review at about -7.5 to -10%, with an earlier warning level around -3%. These are loss limits on the book as a whole. I found no published evidence that pro systematic shops rely on tight per-trade stop-losses.
- Inverse-vol position sizing with a portfolio vol target is standard. It also means the bot sells into volatility spikes, the same way large flows do.

### Gaps
- I found no published concrete position-limit numbers (single name % of NAV) or correlation-limit rules from specific funds.
- I found no primary source on regime filters (for example the 200-day moving average) as used by professionals in 2025-26.
- Per-trade stop-loss usage by systematic funds is not documented in the sources found.

## 3. AI / LLM use in investing, 2025-26

### Takeaway
Large firms mainly use LLMs to speed up research and coding. Man Group's AlphaGPT generates, codes and backtests signals, and several dozen of its signals are approved for live trading. Two Sigma has an "AI-first" internal mandate. Bridgewater runs a dedicated ML-driven fund that made about 11-12% in 2025. Academic and live benchmarks show that LLMs acting as autonomous traders mostly underperform buy-and-hold and manage risk poorly.

### Cited Findings
- Bridgewater's AIA Labs fund (ML-driven, with human oversight) returned about 11% (11.9% per another snippet) in 2025, its first full year. Snippets also say an "AIA Macro Fund" gained 8.1% in H1 2026, matching the flagship's +8.1%. — [Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/behind-bridgewaters-surge); [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/b8c1bc67-24ee-450d-b2be-053130889ae6); [Bridgewater AIA Labs page](https://www.bridgewater.com/aia-labs) (snippets; fund naming is inconsistent across snippets)
- AIA Labs, led by co-CIO Greg Jensen, aims to build "an artificial investor" that matches or beats expert humans. — [Bridgewater](https://www.bridgewater.com/aia-labs) (snippet)
- Man Numeric uses AlphaGPT, an agentic system that mines data, proposes rule-based signals, writes code and backtests. "Several dozen" of its signals have been approved for live trading (PM Ziang Fang). Man Group also partners with Anthropic and uses Claude and Claude Code, mainly for coding efficiency. — [Hedgeweek](https://www.hedgeweek.com/man-group-deploys-agentic-ai-for-quant-signal-discovery/); [Man Group news](https://www.man.com/news-centre/man-group-anthropic-partnership); [AI Street](https://www.ai-street.co/p/man-group-details-its-alphagpt) (snippets)
- Man Group has published "What AI Can (and Can't Yet) Do for Alpha", "How to Stop LLMs Hallucinating" and "AlphaTrend and Agentic Research Workflows" (applying agents to trend following). — [man.com](https://www.man.com/insights/what-ai-can-do-for-alpha); [man.com](https://www.man.com/insights/alphatrend-agentic-research-workflows) (titles only; content blocked)
- Two Sigma requires everyone to use LLMs, for hypothesis generation, research summaries, code generation/debugging and data cleaning. Its message is that success in 2026 depends on "research discipline and organizational capabilities" as much as on the technology. — [Two Sigma, AI in Investment Management: 2026 Outlook Pt II](https://www.twosigma.com/articles/ai-in-investment-management-2026-outlook-part-ii/); [HedgeCo, Apr 2026](https://hedgeco.net/news/04/2026/two-sigmas-ai-first-internal-mandate-the-race-for-operational-alpha-in-the-age-of-frontier-models.html) (snippets)
- A model-risk failure (not an LLM case): a Two Sigma researcher was charged with manipulating models, and Two Sigma refunded about $165m to clients. The snippet dates this to Sept 2025. My recollection is that the SEC settlement was announced in January 2025, so the date is unverified.
- Academic tests: "Can LLM-based Financial Investing Strategies Outperform the Market in Long Run?" tested more than 20 years of data across more than 100 symbols. LLM strategies consistently underperformed buy-and-hold. They were too conservative in bull markets and too aggressive, with weak risk control, in bear markets. — [CryptoBriefing summary](https://cryptobriefing.com/ai-trading-strategies-underperform-buy-and-hold/) (snippet)
- The AI-Trader live benchmark (arXiv 2512.10971, Dec 2025) found that general LLM capabilities "do not readily translate into effective autonomous trading": most agents had poor returns and weak risk management. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.10971v1) (snippet)
- Look-ahead bias is a known problem when backtesting LLMs on historical data, and a benchmark for it exists: Look-Ahead-Bench (arXiv 2601.13770). — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.13770) (title/snippet)

### Inferences
- What has worked is LLMs as research, coding and data assistants inside a disciplined, human-supervised process with backtests, where signals stay rule-based and are approved before going live. What has failed is LLMs making autonomous buy/sell decisions. For a small bot, the professional pattern points to rule-based signals and hard-coded risk limits, with any LLM kept to research, sentiment features or code. LLM outputs should be tested out-of-sample and with care about look-ahead bias.

### Gaps
- I found no audited performance data on retail AI trading tools.
- I could not read Man Group's "what AI can't yet do" content in full.

## 4. Surveys of professional managers (BofA Global FMS)

### Takeaway
Through 2026 fund managers are heavily invested and bullish, with very low cash. Cash hit a record low of 3.3% at one point in 2026 per snippets, and it was 3.5% in August and 3.9% in September 2026. The BofA "Cash Rule" sell signal (cash at or below 4.0%) has been on for much of 2026. Global semiconductors are the most crowded trade in September 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Sept 2026 FMS** (published about 15 Sep 2026): cash rose to 3.9% from 3.5% in August, the largest monthly rise since March 2026. The most crowded trade is "long global semiconductors" (53%). BofA's composite sentiment fell to 7.0 from 8.0. Rising bond yields are testing optimism. The survey covered 170 panelists with $470bn AUM. — [Hedge Fund Tips summary](https://www.hedgefundtips.com/september-2026-bank-of-america-global-fund-manager-survey-results-summary/); [Finvaulta](https://finvaulta.com/research/bank-of-america/global-fund-manager-survey-racing-the-frontier-2026-09-15); [Atrani Capital](https://atranicapital.substack.com/p/september-2026-bank-of-america-global) (snippets)
- Cash path in 2026 per snippets: 4.1% in June, 3.6% in July ("uber-low", triggering a sell signal), 3.5% in August ("sixth-lowest since 1998"). One snippet says cash hit 3.3%, "the lowest in the history of the survey". The date of that record is unclear: an El-Erian post and a LinkedIn post point to around December 2025. — [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4613555-stock-sell-signal-triggered-as-fund-manager-cash-plummets-bofa-survey); [Gate/newswire](https://www.gate.com/en-us/news/detail/bofa-fund-manager-survey-cash-levels-drop-to-36-from-41-triggering-sell-17807047); [El-Erian LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mohamedelerian_markets-investing-investors-activity-7406861254809571329-j9vO) (snippets; the timeline is partly inconsistent)
- The BofA FMS Cash Rule issues a sell signal when cash is at or below 4.0%. — same (snippet)
- BofA's Bull & Bear indicator reached 9.7, described as the "17th sell signal", with markets rising afterwards. The date is unclear. — [ValueAdd VC](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/bofa-bull-bear-indicator-at-97-the-sell-signal-vs-what-markets-did-next) (snippet)

### Inferences
- Positioning is crowded and contrarian indicators are flashing caution. The low-cash sell signal has been on for much of 2026 while markets kept rising, so it is a poor timing tool. It is more useful as a reason to keep risk controls (vol targeting, drawdown tiers) tight than as a signal to exit.
- The most crowded trade (semis) overlaps with 2026 momentum leadership. That is the setup for a sharp factor reversal, as in Jan and Jul/Aug 2026.

### Gaps

- I found no other major manager surveys (e.g., JPM, Goldman hedge fund trend monitor) for 2026.
