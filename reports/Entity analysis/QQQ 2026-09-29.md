> **Words used here.** Unit investment trust: a fund run by a fixed rulebook, with no board. Open-end fund: a fund with a board and an adviser who manage it.
> Distribution: the fund's cash payout. Ex-date: first day a new buyer no longer gets the payout. Rebalance: the index resets weights (quarterly); reconstitution: it resets its member list (December).
> Fast Entry: a quick-join rule for very large new listings. Non-diversified: allowed to hold large amounts of a few companies. Securities lending: the fund lends shares out for a fee, holding cash as security (collateral).
> Opening/Closing Cross: Nasdaq auctions that set the day's first and last price; imbalance data: a live count of unmatched orders before them.
> JOLTS: job-openings survey. ISM: factory and services surveys. FOMC minutes: notes of the Fed's last rate meeting. Shadow setup: its would-be trades are only recorded.
