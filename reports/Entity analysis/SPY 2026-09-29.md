> **Words used here.** NAV: what the fund's holdings are worth per unit. Premium: market price above NAV. Bid/ask spread: gap between best selling and buying prices.
> Ex-date: buy on or after this day and you miss the next payout. Rebalance: the index provider swapping or reweighting companies. Concentration: a few holdings making up much of the fund.
> JOLTS: monthly job-openings count. ISM: monthly surveys of factory and service businesses. GDP third estimate: last revision of quarterly economic output. Personal Income and Outlays: monthly income, spending and price data.
> FOMC minutes: the Fed's written record of its last rate meeting. 8-K: a company's urgent SEC filing. Pause band: a price range; leaving it briefly halts one stock. Shadow setup: its would-be trades are only recorded.
