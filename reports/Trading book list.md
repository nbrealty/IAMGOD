# Essential books for every type of stock trading

No list can hold every book, so this one covers the books traders and investors most often
call essential for each style, 88 in all. Books marked **★** are the must-reads for their
category. Books marked **(playbook)** underpin the strategies our trading system already uses.

**How to buy smart:** start with the 12 in "Buy first", then add a category only once you trade
that style. Most of these are cheap used, on Kindle, or at a library. A few are academic
textbooks or out of print and cost far more, and those are noted.

## Buy first (12 books)

These 12 cover what matters most: the risk of losing money, position sizing, psychology, and the
strategies in our playbook.

1. *The Intelligent Investor*, Benjamin Graham (revised edition with Jason Zweig's commentary)
2. *A Random Walk Down Wall Street*, Burton Malkiel
3. *Market Wizards*, Jack Schwager
4. *Trading in the Zone*, Mark Douglas
5. *Trade Your Way to Financial Freedom*, Van K. Tharp
6. *Trend Following*, Michael Covel (playbook)
7. *Dual Momentum Investing*, Gary Antonacci (playbook)
8. *Short Term Trading Strategies That Work*, Larry Connors & Cesar Alvarez (playbook)
9. *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard*, Mark Minervini (playbook)
10. *The Most Important Thing*, Howard Marks
11. *Reminiscences of a Stock Operator*, Edwin Lefèvre
12. *Evidence-Based Technical Analysis*, David Aronson

## Notes

- Most classics have newer editions. For Graham, get the Zweig commentary edition; for O'Neil,
  Murphy and McMillan, get the latest edition you can find cheaply.
- Many of these have Portuguese editions (for example *O Investidor Inteligente*). Search the
  Portuguese or Brazilian title before buying if you prefer reading in Portuguese.
- The trading bot doesn't need you to buy these. Its rules come from the published research
  behind them. The books are for you, so you can judge what the bot is doing and why.
