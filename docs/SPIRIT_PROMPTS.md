# Spirit Art Prompts — Hollywood & Highland cast

Paste-ready image prompts for the twelve named souls on the first block. Feed
them to ChatGPT (or any image model) one at a time, then save each result into
`public/spirits/` under the exact filename listed. See
[`public/spirits/README.md`](../public/spirits/README.md) for the drop-in spec.

Each character prompt is written to be pasted **after** the Master Style Prefix
below, so every sprite comes out at the same proportions, angle, and lighting —
one cohesive cast standing on the same street. Copy the prefix once, then append
one character block.

---

## Master Style Prefix

> Chibi-proportioned character sprite for a god-game set in a stylized 2026
> Los Angeles. Head roughly one-third of the total body height; short limbs;
> expressive face; **single full-body figure, front-facing at a 3/4 high angle**
> (as if seen from a raised dimetric camera looking down the street), feet
> planted, centered on a **fully transparent background** with a small margin on
> all sides. Clean, crisp pixel-art / cel-shaded style with soft warm daylight
> coming from the upper-left; subtle rim light; no cast shadow on the ground.
> Grounded, realistic **American** streetwear and grooming — **absolutely no
> anime tropes** (no oversized sparkling eyes, no twin-tails, no school
> uniforms, no exaggerated hair colors unless dyed by choice). Square ~512×512
> canvas. Consistent scale and lighting with the rest of the cast.
>
